# Video transcripts by Clipscript

Video transcript generator: transcribe YouTube videos and Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook and X videos to text. Paste a link, get the video transcript with timestamps, and export subtitles and captions as TXT, SRT, VTT or JSON.

## Connect with AI assistants

Our [MCP server](https://github.com/cliptranscript/mcp-server) connects Clipscript to compatible AI clients, with browser sign-in or an API key.

get_transcript transcribes one link and bulk_transcripts up to 10. list_channel_videos lists the latest videos of a channel or profile. list_history, get_saved_transcript and export_transcript reopen saved transcripts as TXT, SRT, VTT or JSON, and get_usage shows your plan allowance.
